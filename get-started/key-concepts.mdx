Five ideas cover almost everything you need to reason about the API. Each links to a page with the full detail.

## Collections

A collection is a named group of videos. Every video belongs to one collection. Pass `collection_id` to search within a known collection and reduce unrelated matches. See [Collections](/video-processing/collections).

## Jobs and the processing lifecycle

Uploading a video creates a processing job. The job moves through `queued → processing → done` or reaches `failed`. A video becomes searchable when the job reaches `done`. See [Processing lifecycle](/video-processing/processing-lifecycle).

## Search signals

Pureframe AI can match visual content, spoken words, scene context, and text visible on screen. Use `modes` to restrict a request when you know which signals matter. See [Search overview](/search/overview).

## Segments, not videos

Search returns timestamped segments grouped by video. Each segment includes `timestamp_start`, `timestamp_end`, and a relevance `score`. It can also include a thumbnail or matching text. One video can contain several matching segments. See [Search results](/search/search-results).

## Credits

Pay as you go accounts are billed per action, deducted from a prepaid credit balance: indexing costs per minute of video, search costs a small flat fee per request. There's no monthly subscription; you top up credits and they draw down as you use the API. Free accounts get a daily search quota and a one-time trial credit instead. See [Usage and Billing](/production/usage-and-billing).
