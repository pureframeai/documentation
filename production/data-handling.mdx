## Ownership

You retain ownership of the content you upload. Pureframe AI processes that content to provide video storage, processing, and search. Pureframe AI does not use uploaded video content to train models.

## What Pureframe AI stores

| Data | Purpose | Retention |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Original video | Playback and processing | Until you delete the video |
| Derived visual data | Visual search and thumbnails | Until you delete the video |
| Transcript segments | Transcript search | Until you delete the video |
| Video and collection metadata | Library management | Until you delete the related resource |
| Credit transaction history | Billing records | 7 years |
| Search logs | Service operation and improvement | Up to 12 months |

Playback and thumbnail URLs expire after approximately one hour. Store the resource ID instead of storing an expiring URL.

## Deleting a video or collection

{/* TODO: Confirm deletion completion timing and backup retention. Document both after owner approval. */}

`DELETE /v1/videos/{video_id}` permanently deletes the video and its derived searchable data. Deleting a collection with `DELETE /v1/collections/{collection_id}` also deletes the videos it contains.

These API operations do not provide an undo or recovery window. Confirm the resource ID before sending a delete request.

## Deleting your account

Start account deletion from **Settings → Account → Danger Zone** in the dashboard. You can cancel during the 30-day grace period. Account deletion is not available through an API key.

After the grace period:

- Videos, collections, and derived searchable data are permanently deleted
- Your user profile is removed
- Credit transaction history is retained but anonymized (required for financial recordkeeping)
- An anonymized record of plan, spend, and tenure is kept for aggregate business metrics. It contains no identifying content

## Cross-border data transfer

Pureframe AI hosts customer content and service data in the United States. Regional data residency is not currently available. If your organization requires in-region processing or storage, [contact us](mailto:support@pureframe.ai) before uploading sensitive content.

## Isolation

API requests are scoped to the account associated with the credential. See [Security](/production/security) for related controls and current limitations.
