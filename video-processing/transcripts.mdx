Pureframe AI transcribes spoken audio during video processing. You do not need to start a separate transcription job.

## Retrieving transcript content

The API does not provide a standalone full-transcript endpoint. Matching transcript text appears in `text_content` on `transcript` segments in [search results](/search/search-results).

A search response contains matching passages rather than a sequential export of everything said. Do not use broad search pagination as a substitute for a guaranteed full transcript.

## Language

Transcript search is optimized for English. Results for other languages may be incomplete. If your content is primarily non-English, test representative recordings before relying on transcript search in production.

## What's not included

- Speaker identification is not available. Transcript segments are not attributed to individual speakers.
- Profanity filtering and automatic redaction are not available.
- You cannot edit transcript text through the API.

## Relationship to search

See [Transcript search](/search/transcript) to query spoken content and [Combined queries](/search/combined-queries) to combine transcript and visual signals.
