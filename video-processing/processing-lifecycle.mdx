title: Processing lifecycle
description: Understand when an uploaded video becomes searchable and how to track it.
---

Pureframe AI processes each uploaded video asynchronously. The upload response includes a `job_id` that you can use to track progress.

## What processing prepares

During processing, Pureframe AI prepares searchable information from:

- Visual content
- Spoken audio
- Scene context, when available on your plan
- Text visible on screen

<Note>
  Scene labels and captions are available on Pay as you go and Enterprise plans.
</Note>

## Track progress

Poll `GET /v1/jobs/{job_id}` until the job reaches `done` or `failed`. A video becomes searchable only after its job reaches `done`.

Use exponential backoff when polling. You can also subscribe to `job.completed` and `job.failed` webhooks. See [Processing status](/video-processing/processing-status).

## Processing time

Processing time depends on video length, format, content, and current demand. Track the job rather than relying on a fixed completion estimate.

## Handle a failed job

If a job reaches `failed`, re-upload the video to create a new job. If the same file fails again, verify that its container and codec are supported before contacting support.

See [Upload videos](/video-processing/upload-videos) for supported formats and [Errors](/production/errors) for API error details.
